About Vindex

We keep operations teams current on AI.

Vindex exists because the way companies use AI changes every quarter — and scattered articles, slide decks and half-remembered webinars can't keep up. We turn that moving target into one calm, structured, always-current knowledge base.

Our mission

Most organisations know AI matters. Far fewer have a single, trusted place that tells their people what to actually do about it — in language their teams understand, updated as the field moves.

Vindex is that place. A sterile, bank-grade platform where leadership sets the direction and every employee finds exactly the guidance meant for them — nothing more, nothing less.

Trust first

Security and clarity before features. The platform should feel like a bank, not a startup.

Always current

Knowledge that's out of date is worse than none. We keep every module living.

Radically simple

One structure, two tiers, no clutter. Anyone can find what they need in seconds.
